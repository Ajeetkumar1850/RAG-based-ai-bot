PLACEMENT PROCESS
========================================

The Training & Placement Cell:
1. Conducts workshops and training activities
2. Provides soft skills development
3. Offers interview preparation support
4. Facilitates industry connections
5. Organizes campus recruitment drives
6. Provides career guidance and counseling

Students receive:
- Personal growth opportunities
- Holistic learning environment
- Constant support and encouragement
- Platform to connect with industry
- Confidence building activities
